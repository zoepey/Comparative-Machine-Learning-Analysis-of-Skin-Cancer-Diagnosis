In this project, we aim to predict the “cancer” status and the relation of 50 predictors to cancer risk of an individual based on one’s demographic, environmental factors, sun exposure,
and other lifestyle behaviors.

Among these variables, we believe that ultraviolet (UV) exposure is the primary cause of melanoma and other skin cancers, with indoor tanning before age 35 increasing melanoma risk
by 59% (American Academy of Dermatology, 2024). Geographic location also contributes, as individuals in southern or high-altitude regions experience stronger UV radiation. Based on the
background information provided, men face nearly double the melanoma mortality rate of women and racial disparities, with later diagnoses and poorer outcomes among people with
darker skin (CDC, 2024). These background insights motivate our investigation of predictors of cancer status.
